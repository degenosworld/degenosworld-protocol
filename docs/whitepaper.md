# DegenOS Whitepaper

## Vision

Memecoins are a culture first and a market second, yet the tooling around them treats every token as a blank spreadsheet. DegenOS imagines tokens that carry their own behaviour: some act on their own, some assist the people behind them, some are bound by rules the chain enforces, and some route their rewards to the people who made them matter.

## Mission

Give every Solana token creator and community a single, verifiable layer for operating, protecting and growing a token, with every action observable on-chain and every claim traceable to a record.

## Systems

- **Agents**: tokens with a dedicated wallet and character that act inside fixed limits.
- **Assistants**: tooling that prepares and flags, while the operator keeps every signature.
- **Guardrails**: holding, transfer and selling constraints fixed at mint.
- **Handles**: reward routing to a public social handle, claimable by its owner.

## Roadmap

1. **Foundation**: public site, live market board, contract slot, register of external facts.
2. **Tooling**: assistant briefings, holder and bundle analysis, alerting.
3. **Autonomy**: agent runtime with bounded treasury actions and a public decision log.
4. **Network**: guardrail templates, handle routing, and integrations with launch venues.
